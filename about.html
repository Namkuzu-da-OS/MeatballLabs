Escape from the Matrix

12 years ago I was able to escape the matrix for a short time. I was a full-time trader and had a glimpse into what a life free of work and need for money is. I spent that time researching our ancient past, seeking wisdom and truth and understanding what it means to be in control of your life. I felt like a king, a pharaoh. Everyone seemed so weird to me, rushing about in their stimulant-driven lives. So I'm here to tell you that I had that life and it did not lead to negative things. I went inside on a very deep level, so deep the things that I found took decades to believe. I was not able to stay though, because the more I gained, the more I had to lose and the pressure finally threw me out of balance and literally drove me into the ground.

The Fall and Rise

From sitting at my desk I couldn't even stand up. Going to the mailbox was a challenge. Finally I found myself one day unable to pick my own body up off of the ground—I had become that weak. So I started to focus on my health: walking to the mailbox, walking to the bridge at the corner one block away, walking to the store to get my food with only a backpack and eventually joining a gym and getting in shape. From there I moved to a place of great beauty and began hiking as I still had money to live off of and did not need to instantly get a job. As I settled in this new place and my money ran out, I began to come back into my career of technology as it's all I've ever known. I've spent many years down from the mountain and into the city steeped in a purely material gain lifestyle, unable to fit in knowing what I knew.

The Launchpad - Our Collective Journey

So here I am, on my launchpad back into freedom, to escape the matrix once again and to never come back. Ancient knowledge, futuristic technology, woven together in a unique experience that has been a gift to live.

Nothing is going to stop me this time and when I make it I will use whatever power over the material world that I gain from this journey to use the rest of my life to help everybody cross this bridge between what was and the future that is so beautiful that is about to unlock before us.

Don't let anybody tell you you can't, don't let anybody tell you to do it their way and not yours. Take the time to be in nature, to hear the wind, to hear the birds and feel the sun. You will always find your path and who you are out there. And when you do, bring it back and share it.

Peace and prosperity to every living being on this planet, in this galaxy and to the edges of all existence. No matter your religion, philosophy, my arms and mind will always be open to you. I don't have all the answers, but what feels right is that there is a life force of some kind and if you seek it you will find it and if you harmonize with it it is very good.
